How to read these observations

Zero means the source reported zero. Unknown means a field or item was not reported reliably. A country has its own source timestamp; a new collection does not renew an old source report.

Changes compare adjacent published reports with advancing source times. They are net reported changes, not sales counts or confirmed restocks. Matching cached reports retain the last supported change and its original interval, labelled as the last change rather than a new change. Gaps may hide changes. Prices are reported shop prices in Torn dollars, not resale prices or profit estimates.

This page checks for a newer snapshot every 30 seconds while visible. Publication, collection and source timestamps describe different delays. Your device clock is used for age labels. Forecasts and restock estimates are not published here.

All timestamps UTC. Stock observations and operations share TornRedeye hosting; source collection runs independently.
